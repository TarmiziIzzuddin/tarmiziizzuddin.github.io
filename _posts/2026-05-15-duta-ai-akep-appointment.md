I am honoured to announce my appointment as **Duta AI (AI Ambassador)** under the **Akademi Kepimpinan Pendidikan Tinggi (AKEPT)**, Ministry of Higher Education Malaysia, effective **15 May 2026 – 31 December 2027**.

This role involves promoting AI adoption and literacy across Malaysia's higher education institutions, contributing to the nation's digital transformation agenda, and supporting AKEPT's leadership development programmes in the field of artificial intelligence.
