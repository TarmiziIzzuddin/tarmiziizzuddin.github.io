I am pleased to share that I am a **Co-Researcher** on a newly awarded industry research grant from **Petronas Research Sdn. Bhd.**, totalling **RM 288,750.00**, for the project:

> **"Optimal Energy Prediction System Simulator for Compressors Network in Oil and Gas Facilities"**
> (Reference: INDUSTRI/PRSB/2024/FTKM/I00099)

The project develops **energy-forecasting algorithms** for motor compressor networks using both **classical deep learning methods** and **generative AI methods**, supporting Petronas' operational efficiency and sustainability goals in oil and gas facilities.

This grant also supports the ongoing **Master (M.Sc) supervision** of Humam Nazir on a related thesis topic. See the [portfolio entry](/portfolio/petronas-compressor-energy-forecasting/) and [CV grants section](/cv/) for details.
