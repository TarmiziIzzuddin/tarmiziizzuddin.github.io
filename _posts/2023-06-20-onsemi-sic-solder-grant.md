I am pleased to share that I am a **Co-Researcher** on an awarded international research grant totalling **RM 215,000.00**, for the project:

> **"Predictive Modelling Using AI for SiC Solder Interface at Packaging Level"**
> (Reference: ANTARABANGSA-SCI/2023/FKE/A00056)

In collaboration with **OnSemi Sdn. Bhd.**, this project developed **deep-learning image segmentation algorithms** to detect **solder voids and defects** in X-ray images of **Silicon Carbide (SiC) modules** at the packaging level — supporting next-generation power semiconductor manufacturing quality control.

The grant also supported a **1-year industrial research attachment** to OnSemi (2023–2024) and produced a [2024 IEEE IICAIET conference paper](/publication/2024-solder-void-detection/). See the [portfolio entry](/portfolio/onsemi-sic-solder-void-detection/) and [CV grants section](/cv/) for details.
