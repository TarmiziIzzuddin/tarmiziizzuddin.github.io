I am honoured to announce my appointment as **Google Ambassador** under the **Ministry of Higher Education (MoHE) Malaysia**, effective **25 May 2026**.

This appointment, in collaboration with **awantec** and **Google for Education**, recognises the use of Google's AI and education tools — including **NotebookLM** and **Gemini** — to advance teaching, learning, and digital transformation across Malaysia's higher education institutions.
