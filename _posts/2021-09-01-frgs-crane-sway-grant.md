I am pleased to share that I am a **Co-Researcher** on an awarded **FRGS (Fundamental Research Grant Scheme)** from the Ministry of Higher Education Malaysia, totalling **RM 113,600.00**, for the project:

> **"An Adaptive Frequency-Free Command Smoothing of Crane Sway Control with Multimode Dynamics"**
> (Reference: FRGS/1/2021/FKE/F00464)

FRGS is one of Malaysia's most competitive national research grant schemes, funding high-quality fundamental research across disciplines. This project addresses **adaptive control of crane sway** — a long-standing challenge in industrial automation, port logistics, and construction — using **frequency-free command smoothing techniques** that handle multimode dynamics.

The work contributes to UTeM's strength in **control systems engineering** and complements the department's broader portfolio in **rehabilitation and assistive technology (REAT)** and **industrial AI**. See the [CV grants section](/cv/) for the full grants list.
