## Overview

Awarded in 2024, this **industry-funded research grant** from **Petronas Research Sdn. Bhd.** (Reference: INDUSTRI/PRSB/2024/FTKM/I00099) supports the development of an **Optimal Energy Prediction System Simulator for Compressor Networks in Oil and Gas Facilities**. The total grant value is **RM 288,750.00**.

The project develops **energy-forecasting algorithms** for motor compressor networks using both **classical deep learning methods** and **generative AI methods**, supporting predictive maintenance and energy optimisation across Petronas upstream and downstream operations.

## Project Scope

* **Domain:** Industrial energy management, oil & gas, predictive analytics.
* **Methodologies:** Classical deep learning, generative AI, time-series forecasting.
* **Application:** Energy forecasting for motor compressor networks in oil and gas facilities.
* **Role:** Co-Researcher.
* **Period:** 2024 – ongoing.

## Significance

Compressor networks are among the most energy-intensive assets in oil and gas operations. Accurate energy prediction enables better scheduling, reduced wastage, and lower carbon footprint — directly supporting Petronas' sustainability and operational efficiency goals.
