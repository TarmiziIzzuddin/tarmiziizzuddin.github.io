## Overview

Registered in **2024** with the UTeM e-CV portal under Intellectual Properties, this **Copyright** records the **"Optimal Demand Response Simulator: A Process"** — a software/process IP for AI-driven energy demand response, co-invented under the Petronas compressor-network research engagement.

* **Type:** Copyright
* **Role:** Co-Inventor
* **Year:** 2024

## Related Research

This IP supports the [**Petronas Compressor Network Energy Forecasting** grant (RM 288,750, 2024)](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=u7-6P2gAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao), which develops AI-based energy forecasting for motor compressor networks in oil and gas facilities.
