## Overview

Awarded in 2023, this **international research grant** (Reference: ANTARABANGSA-SCI/2023/FKE/A00056) totalling **RM 215,000.00** funded the project **"Predictive Modelling Using AI for SiC Solder Interface at Packaging Level"** in collaboration with **OnSemi Sdn. Bhd.**

The project developed **deep-learning image segmentation algorithms** to detect **solder voids and defects** in X-ray images of **Silicon Carbide (SiC) modules** at the packaging level, supporting next-generation power semiconductor manufacturing quality control.

## Project Scope

* **Domain:** Computer vision, industrial quality control, semiconductor packaging.
* **Methodologies:** Deep learning image segmentation, X-ray image analysis.
* **Application:** Solder void and defect detection in SiC power modules.
* **Role:** Co-Researcher.
* **Period:** 2023 – 2024.

## Industrial Attachment

The grant also supported a **1-year industrial research attachment** to OnSemi Sdn. Bhd. (2023–2024), where the team deployed and validated the algorithms in a real production environment.
