## Overview

I am honoured to have been appointed as **Duta AI (AI Ambassador)** by the **Akademi Kepimpinan Pendidikan Tinggi (AKEPT)**, under the **Kementerian Pendidikan Tinggi Malaysia (Ministry of Higher Education)**, effective **15 May 2026 to 31 December 2027**.

## Scope of Role

- Promote **AI adoption and literacy** across Malaysian higher education institutions
- Support **AKEPT's leadership development programmes** in artificial intelligence
- Contribute to the **nation's digital transformation agenda** through AI education and consultancy
- Serve as a **bridge between academia, industry, and government** in AI-related initiatives

## Significance

This appointment recognises expertise in AI and machine learning, and provides a platform to drive meaningful AI integration in Malaysia's higher education ecosystem. It aligns with the Malaysian government's broader AI roadmap and digital economy blueprint (MyDigital).

## Duration

**15 May 2026 – 31 December 2027**
